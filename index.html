Flower Care Tips

Make your blooms last longer with these simple, expert-approved care instructions.

1. Trim the Stems: Cut 1-2 inches off the stems at a 45-degree angle before placing them in a vase. This allows for better water absorption.

2. Fresh Water: Change the water every two days. Always use lukewarm water as flowers find it easier to drink than cold water.

3. Keep it Cool: Place your bouquet away from direct sunlight, heating vents, and ripening fruit (which releases gases that age flowers).

4. Remove Foliage: Ensure no leaves are submerged in the water to prevent bacterial growth and keep the water clear.
